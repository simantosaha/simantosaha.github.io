Research

My research focuses on applying advanced signal processing and artificial intelligence techniques to develop solutions for biomedical applications, including brain-computer interfaces and the diagnosis and prognosis of various clinical conditions. One of the key translational challenges in this area is the variability of biomedical signals due to physiological, hardware, and environmental factors. The variability hinders the generalisability of an algorithm over time and across users. My research investigates the sources of variability, explores potential markers that describe variability, and develops reproducible pattern recognition solutions.
